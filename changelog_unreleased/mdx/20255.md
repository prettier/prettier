#### Keep inline MDX tags in their paragraph (#20255 by @Suwanwa)

With `proseWrap: "always"`, inline tags could move to the start of a line and become separate JSX blocks. This also made repeated formatting insert an extra blank line. The formatter now wraps before the preceding word instead.

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
```mdx
<!-- Input -->
123456789 123456789 123456789 123456789 123456789 123456789 123456789 123456789 <tag /> words words

<!-- Prettier stable -->
123456789 123456789 123456789 123456789 123456789 123456789 123456789 123456789
<tag /> words words

<!-- Prettier main -->
123456789 123456789 123456789 123456789 123456789 123456789 123456789
123456789 <tag /> words words
```
