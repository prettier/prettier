#### Support directives in MDX (#19343 by @fisker and @jonesfionn101-dotcom)

Directives (`:::note`, `::leaf`, `:text`) are now parsed and printed instead of
being broken up. Their attributes are kept exactly as written, including
shorthands, quoting and character references.

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
```mdx
{/* Input */}
Text before :abbr[HTML]{title="HyperText Markup Language"} and text after.

:::note[label with {braces}]{#id}
body
:::

{/* Prettier stable */}
Text before :abbr\[HTML]\{title="HyperText Markup Language"} and text after.

:::note\[label with \{braces}]\{#id}
body
:::

{/* Prettier main */}
Text before :abbr[HTML]{title="HyperText Markup Language"} and text after.

:::note[label with {braces}]{#id}
body
:::
```
