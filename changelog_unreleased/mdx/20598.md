#### Honor MDX ignore comments with trailing whitespace (#20598 by @porada)

Spaces or tabs after a standalone MDX ignore comment could cause Prettier to reformat the next block or the contents of an ignored range.

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
```mdx
{/* Input (two spaces after the ignore comment) */}

{/* prettier-ignore */}  

<Button label='Save' />

{/* Prettier stable */}

{/* prettier-ignore */}

<Button label="Save" />

{/* Prettier main */}

{/* prettier-ignore */}
<Button label='Save' />
```
