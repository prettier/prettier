#### Preserve line break or space between/around Chinese or Japanese characters in HTML (#20290 by @tats-u)

In Firefox, newlines around or between Japanese/Chinese characters might be rendered simply by being removed instead of being replaced with spaces. For example, the following input is rendered as "文字 文字 word word word word 文字 文字文字 文字！word word word word". Such ambiguous newlines, which are handled differently in other browsers, and spaces that risk being deleted if converted to newlines, are now preserved as is without being converted to the other.

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
```html
<!-- Input -->
<p lang="ja">
  文字 文字
  word word
  word word
  文字 文字
  文字 文字！
  word word
  word word
</p>

<!-- Prettier stable -->
<p lang="ja">
  文字 文字 word word word word 文字 文字 文字 文字！ word word word word
</p>

<!-- Prettier main -->
<p lang="ja">
  文字 文字 word word word word 文字 文字
  文字 文字！
  word word word word
</p>
```
