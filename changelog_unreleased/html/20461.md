#### Keep whitespace-only content of whitespace-sensitive elements (#20461 by @MIbtehajNasar)

Whitespace is significant in `<pre>`, `<textarea>` and other whitespace-sensitive elements, but Prettier dropped their content when it was whitespace-only.

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
```html
<!-- Input -->
<pre> </pre>
<textarea name="u">  </textarea>

<!-- Prettier stable -->
<pre></pre>
<textarea name="u"> </textarea>

<!-- Prettier main -->
<pre> </pre>
<textarea name="u">  </textarea>
```
