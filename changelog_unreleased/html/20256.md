#### Preserve CDATA content in HTML and Angular templates (#20256 by @splincode)

Prettier now preserves whitespace and line breaks inside CDATA sections instead of formatting their contents as regular text. This applies to both the `html` and `angular` parsers.

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
```html
<!-- Input -->
<![CDATA[<app-alert>
  Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet consectetur adipisicing elit.
</app-alert>]]>

<!-- Prettier stable (--parser angular) -->
<![CDATA[<app-alert> Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet consectetur adipisicing elit.
</app-alert>]]>

<!-- Prettier main (--parser angular) -->
<![CDATA[<app-alert>
  Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet consectetur adipisicing elit.
</app-alert>]]>
```
