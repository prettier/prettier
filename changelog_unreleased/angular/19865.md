#### Normalize spaces in multi-binding `@for` let clauses (#19865 by @santhiprakash)

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
```html
<!-- Input -->
@for (item of items; track item.id; let i=$index, f=$first) {}

<!-- Prettier stable -->
@for (item of items; track item.id; let i=$index, f=$first) {}

<!-- Prettier main -->
@for (item of items; track item.id; let i = $index, f = $first) {}
```
