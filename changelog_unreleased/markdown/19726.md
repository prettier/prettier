#### Fix extra `>` inserted before escaped `\>` in blockquotes (#19726 by @LHMQ878)

Escaped angle brackets inside blockquotes are no longer prefixed with an extra `>`, including on
continuation lines and when other content precedes the escape.

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
```markdown
<!-- Input -->
> \>

> **test**\>

> \>
> \>

> *text* > \>

<!-- Prettier stable -->
> >\>

> **test**>\>

> \> >\>

> _text_ > >\>

<!-- Prettier main -->
> \>

> **test**\>

> \>
> \>

> _text_ > \>
```
