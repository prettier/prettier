#### Fix extra `>` inserted before escaped `\>` in blockquotes (#19726 by @LHMQ878)

Escaped angle brackets inside blockquotes are no longer prefixed with an extra `>`.

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
```markdown
<!-- Input -->
> test\>

> **test**\>

> \>

<!-- Prettier stable -->
> test\>

> **test**>\>

> >\>

<!-- Prettier main -->
> test\>

> **test**\>

> \>
```
