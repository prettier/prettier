#### Preserve newline around Chinese/Japanese characters not around Korean letters in all proseWrap settings (#20143 by @tats-u)

When rendering Markdown, it usually goes through a process of being converted to HTML, and the line breaks within the document are often preserved as they are. In HTML, line breaks between Chinese/Japanese characters are interpreted differently depending on the browser. Firefox removes such line breaks in accordance with the latest CSS standards, whereas other browsers follow older CSS standards and convert them to spaces, just like line breaks around other characters.

Prettier is merely a formatter, so deciding on one specific meaning for such ambiguous line breaks goes beyond its responsibility.
